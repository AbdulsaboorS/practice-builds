    Policy guardrails

    What Aria may and may not do

    Guardrails are checked before every action, not after. When one fires, the caller hears the policy, not a vague refusal.

    • Freeze and replace a card

      Allowed at any time once the caller is verified. No supervisor approval needed.

      Allowed
    • Two factor verification required

      POL-201: no account action until two factors pass. A failed code pauses the workflow.

      Enforced
    • Fee waivers capped at $15

      POL-114, Sec 3.2: courtesy credits up to $15 per case. Anything higher needs a supervisor.

      Standing
    • Full card numbers and PINs

      Never disclosed or collected by voice. New card details arrive only after app activation.

      Blocked
    • Disputes over $100 escalate

      POL-308: unauthorized charges above $100 go to a fraud specialist. No refund may be promised first.

      Standing
