tour-lens

Built for EliseAI. Concept demo, not affiliated. The property, units and people are made up.

An AI tour guide that sees what you see. During a self-guided apartment tour you point your phone at something, tap, and ask about it. It answers out loud from that unit's actual records and shows where the answer came from. If what the camera sees doesn't match the records, it says so and flags it for the leasing team.

I was researching the verticals EliseAI operates in and honestly expected to find this somewhere. I didn't, so I built it.
