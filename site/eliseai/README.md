# tour-lens

**What it is:** an AI tour guide that sees what you see. During a self-guided apartment tour you point your phone at something and ask about it. It answers out loud from that unit's records, shows the source, and flags anything that doesn't match the records for the leasing team.

**Built for:** EliseAI. Concept demo, not affiliated. The property, units and people are made up.

**What it proves:** I can spot a gap in a product's real workflow and ship a working version of the fix, not just a pitch.

**Live:** https://tour-lens.feedback-signal.workers.dev (needs an access code, ask me)
**Code and write-up:** https://github.com/AbdulsaboorS/tour-lens

The app runs on a Cloudflare Worker with AI models, so it can't live in this static site. This folder holds a landing page and the 1 minute demo video.
